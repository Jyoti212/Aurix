Our Philosophy

Four decades of building the same way, on purpose.

What we believe shapes how every Aurix project gets planned, priced and handed over.

Started in 1983, still run the same way.

Aurix Enterprises began as a small Pune-based construction outfit in 1983, working on individual homes across the western suburbs. Over four decades, that same attention to detail scaled into townships, gated communities and premium residences — without changing the fundamentals: clear titles, honest timelines, and construction that holds up.

Today, over 4,000 families call an Aurix address home, across more than 114 delivered landmarks and 7 million square feet of built space. The company is still led with the same philosophy it started with — building relationships, not just buildings.
